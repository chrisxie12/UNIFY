GCTU Study Circle

A complete planning document for a student collaboration platform built for Ghana Communication Technology University.

01

Site Name

GCTU Study Circle

I chose GCTU Study Circle because it clearly tells visitors what the platform is about. "GCTU" identifies the university, and "Study Circle" suggests a group of students who gather around a shared academic interest. The name is short, easy to remember, and works well for a .com or .org domain. It also sounds welcoming — like any student can join and find their people.

02

Site Purpose

GCTU Study Circle is a student-built platform designed to help GCTU students connect over academics. The website lets students find or create study groups for specific courses, share lecture notes and past questions, organise revision timetables before exams, and discover classmates who are taking the same subjects. It also includes a resource library where students can upload and download materials. The goal is to make studying feel less isolated and more collaborative — especially for freshmen who may not know anyone in their classes yet.

03

Target Audience

This platform is built for undergraduate students at Ghana Communication Technology University. Our primary audience includes:

  • ☾
    Freshmen who are still finding their way around and want study partners in their first-year courses.
  • ★
    Continuing students looking for course-specific groups for upper-level classes like Networking or Database Systems.
  • ⚖
    Exam-season students who need accountability partners for revision before mid-semester and final exams.
  • ✎
    Department-based learners who want to filter groups by department to stay focused on their programme requirements.
04

Scenarios

These are real questions a student might ask when visiting the site for the first time:

1

"I just started my first year and I don't know anyone in my Programming class. How do I find other students taking the same course so we can study together?"

A freshman looking for classmates in a specific first-year subject.

2

"Exams are in three weeks and I really need a revision group that meets consistently. Is there a way to see which groups are actively meeting right now?"

A continuing student who wants to find active, ongoing study circles near exam period.

3

"I want to start a study group for my Database Systems class. Can I create a group, set a meeting schedule, and invite people from my department?"

A student who wants to take initiative and create their own group rather than join an existing one.

05

Color Schema

I picked a palette that feels professional and modern, with a strong blue anchor and warm amber accents for energy.

Primary #4F46E5

Used for all headings, navigation links, buttons, and accent borders throughout the site.

Secondary #F59E0B

Used for badges, highlights, call-to-action accents, and icon backgrounds.

Background #F8FAFC

Main page background to keep things light and easy on the eyes.

Surface #FFFFFF

Card backgrounds and content containers for clean contrast.

Text #111827

Primary body text and paragraph content for strong readability.

Muted #6B7280

Secondary text, metadata labels, and less prominent information.

09

Content Outline

01

Home

Hero banner with a tagline and search bar. Below that, featured study groups, upcoming revision sessions, and a call-to-action to create your own group. Quick stats showing how many students and groups are active.

02

Study Groups

A searchable and filterable directory of all study groups. Each card shows the course name, meeting schedule, member count, and a join button. Users can filter by department, course code, or meeting type.

03

Courses

Course catalogue organised by department. Each course page lists the groups available for that subject and any related resources. Students can see which courses have the most active study circles.

04

Resources

A shared library of academic materials. Students can upload lecture notes, past exam papers, study guides, and revision checklists. Resources are tagged by course code and department for easy searching.

05

About

Information about the platform, the team behind it, and how it helps GCTU students. Includes testimonials from students who have used the platform to improve their grades.

06

Contact

A simple contact form, an FAQ section covering common questions, and links to social media channels. Students can also report issues or suggest new features.
