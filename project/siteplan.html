GCTU Study Circle

The name GCTU Study Circle was chosen because it directly reflects the purpose of the platform: connecting students of Ghana Communication Technology University in collaborative academic circles. The word "Circle" suggests a community where knowledge flows freely, members support one another, and learning happens together. It is concise, memorable, and clearly communicates the platform's focus on peer-to-peer academic collaboration at GCTU.

Site Purpose

GCTU Study Circle is a dedicated academic collaboration platform for students of Ghana Communication Technology University. The website enables students to discover and join study groups based on their courses and departments, create personalized study circles for exam preparation, share academic resources such as lecture notes and past questions, schedule revision sessions with classmates, and connect with peers who share the same academic goals. By fostering a structured yet flexible environment for group learning, the platform helps students improve their understanding of course material, stay accountable, and build lasting academic relationships that enhance their overall university experience.

Target Audience

The platform serves undergraduate students at Ghana Communication Technology University, including freshmen seeking orientation and study partners, continuing students looking for course-specific groups, students preparing for mid-semester and end-of-semester examinations, and those who want to connect with classmates in their department. The audience ranges from first-year students navigating new academic territory to final-year students organising revision sessions for capstone projects and final exams.

Color Schema

Primary #4F46E5

Used for headings, navigation links, buttons, accents, and interactive elements throughout the site.

Secondary #F59E0B

Used for highlights, badges, icons, call-to-action accents, and emphasis elements.

Background #F8FAFC

Used as the main page background to keep the layout clean and light.

Surface #FFFFFF

Used for card backgrounds, content areas, and elevated UI components.

Text #111827

Used for body copy and primary text content for optimal readability.

Muted #6B7280

Used for secondary text, metadata, placeholders, and less prominent information.

Typography

Headings — Poppins

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog

Poppins is used for all headings (h1–h6), navigation items, and prominent titles. Its geometric, clean structure gives the site a modern and academic feel.

Body — Karla

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog

Karla is used for all body text, paragraphs, descriptions, and UI labels. Its excellent readability at various sizes makes it ideal for content-heavy pages.

Content Outline

Home

Hero banner with search bar, featured study groups, upcoming revision sessions, recent resources, and a call-to-action to join or create a group.

Study Groups

Searchable and filterable list of all study groups. Each group displays course name, member count, meeting schedule, and a join button.

Courses

Course catalogue organised by department. Each course page lists available study groups and resources specific to that subject.

Resources

Repository of shared academic materials including lecture slides, past exam papers, study guides, and student-created summaries.

About

Information about the platform, its mission, the team behind it, and how students can get the most out of the study circle community.

Contact

Contact form, FAQ section, and links to social media channels for support and community engagement.
